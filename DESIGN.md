# Company design / 企业官网设计

Reference: https://designmd.ai/heerentanna/clutch-clean-trust (the reference page labels its license as MIT).
参考：https://designmd.ai/heerentanna/clutch-clean-trust （参考页面标注 MIT）。

This is an independent implementation inspired by clear typography, restrained blue-green accents and whitespace. Do not copy the Clutch brand, pages or business data.
本工程为独立实现，借鉴清晰排版、克制蓝绿色和留白，不复制 Clutch 品牌、页面或业务数据。

- Use white surfaces, dark blue-gray text and blue-green accents. Exact values live in `src/styles/tokens.css`.
  使用白色页面、深蓝灰文字和蓝绿色强调色；准确值以 `src/styles/tokens.css` 为准。

- On wide screens, use a split hero, three service columns and two project columns. Stack content and collapse navigation on narrow screens.
  宽屏采用左右首屏、三列服务和两列项目；窄屏纵向排列并折叠导航。

- Keep headings structured and body text comfortably spaced. Prioritize content over decoration.
  标题保持清晰结构，正文保留充足行距，内容优先于装饰。

- Use local images with recorded provenance. Never present stock photos as actual client projects.
  使用记录来源的本地图片，不将图库照片宣称为真实客户项目。

- Use fine borders and modest rounding. Avoid large gradients, glow and elaborate animation.
  使用细边框和适量圆角，避免大面积渐变、发光和复杂动画。

- Preserve native button/link behavior and visible focus. Respect reduced-motion preferences; do not add custom keyboard navigation.
  保留按钮与链接的原生行为和可见焦点，遵循减少动态效果偏好，不添加自定义键盘导航。

- Use a fixed light brand theme independent of the host console theme.
  使用独立于主站控制台的固定浅色品牌主题。

- Keep demo labels until real project information is supplied. Do not invent client logos, testimonials, certifications or performance figures.
  提供真实资料前保留演示标记，不编造客户 Logo、评价、认证或业绩。
